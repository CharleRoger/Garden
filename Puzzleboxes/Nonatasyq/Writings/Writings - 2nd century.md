
By Sertaxa, a largely secular philosopher from early on in the century:

> And there are those who left the temples, meeting in normal houses. It is said they worship not "Fecul", but "One". They respond that they worship the one divinity [xalca] common to all gods and thus honor all gods. Much suspicion has been directed towards them, especially from the totyq, for they meet only among their own kind and sing secret [[nona|songs]] [nonaq] of praise no one else may hear. Yet they are invariably from among the families of the Wise, and in good standing with the King.

By Purkaferrum, a tot from [[Serquky]].

> Consider two men who know as much as the other. The first man knows a lot about a few things, say metalworking, whereas the second knows little about a lot of things. The first man is valued, but the second is useless for the city, as for everything he can do, you can find someone else to do it better. Indeed, everyone has a role in a city and should focus on their own role instead of other pursuits. This kind of fixation is the source of the noble quality of a man. A leader is made noble by fixation on governing, a tot by fixation on a god, and a hunter by fixation on hunting. The reason why some roles are called more noble than others is due to how intensely one can fixate on the task. A leader, having servants to take care of his things, is able to fixate more deeply leading, but a hunter must concern himself with many things outside of hunting. 
> 
> For totyq, the most noble practice is on fixating on a specific god. A tot here is made noble by fixating on Serqu, whereas a tot from Taspi is noble when he fixates on Pelyr [unusual spelling of Pilur]. Someone may think that fixating on two gods is holier [xal] than fixating on one, but it is not, just like the second man is not nobler than the first. Instead, totyq of Serqu, practice the following: each morning, sit still, and try to ignore everything except for Serqu. Give half of what you own and half of what you gain as xal to Serqu. During thunder, go outside and sing praise to Serqu, for he has given you rain. Truly, Serqu is generous to those who are generous to him.

By Raxa, who installed as king of [[Feculky]] after the first [[Totky-Feculky wars|Totky-Feculky war]]:

>Given in the 15th year of King Raxa of [[Feculky]] and approved by the totyq. Recite this in the town square and next to the doors of the nonatashouses: all nonatasyq must leave [same word as in "leave the city"] singing and praise Fecul in the temple or leave the city. After 12 days, any nonatas singing in the city of Fecul will be xal in the name of Fecul. For they [singular] have given Fecul nothing, and have brought great wrath upon Feculky, they will be given to Fecul. Should anyone stay or help a nonatas stay, they [singular] will be struck by all my forces.
