The following two were given during the Feculky uprising:

> Approved by the totyq and given in the 30th year of King Raxa of Feculky. Having seen the recent happenings in Kypky and the will of the city, we raise tot Tegen as the Tot Cenukki in Feculky, the first of the totyq. We order all soldiers and to swear an oath to Tegen, such that any soldier who disobeys him shall be xal and shall given to Fecul. Tegen's holy [xal] face shall be veiled and must not be seen by anyone but another tot. Should this rule be broken, we decree to punish the offender: by the perforation of the eyes if accidental, by death if intentional. For having seen by accident, the eyes are xal, but having seen on purpose, the mind too is xal. May the people be loyal to him and thank Fecul for his wisdom [pun; Tegen means wisdom].

> Given in the first year of Tegen, Tot Cenukki. Recite this to the soldiers, so that they may avoid the wrath of Fecul should they encounter a nonatas:
> If the nonatas plays the flute 
>    cut the flute in half
> If the nonatas plucks strings
>    cut the strings in half
> If the nonatas hits bars
>    cut the bars in half
> If the nonatas sings
>    cut the throat in half
> Do this and no more. Always act gently so as to not encourage them in their ways, for they may yet leave them. But if they do not heed your warnings, act firmly and do not regret it.

The following letter was sent in the aftermath of the uprising.

> Commander Pilurlaxo in Kyxal greets the Totyq Cenukkiq in Kypky with obedience and praises the holy siblings, both here and there. It was given that the totyq of Fecul were to be slain were they not to make a sacrifice to Pilur, Kyp, or Serqu, as a sign of regret of their former ways. Many did so, and were spared. Still, most totyq, including the so-called Tot Cenukki, refused and have been put to death. This has caused great unease in the citizens, some of whom are ready to follow their former leaders to the grave and have attacked our forces. We have requested reinforcements, but they have yet to arrive. If you can do anything to hasten them, we ask it to be done.
> 
> Some of these citizens are led by totyq who claimed to renounce Fecul, but continue worship in secret. Other totyq escaped entirely, never having been brought before us, for the citizens refused to identify them as totyq and falsely swore they were not. We punish such lies harshly and have made this known to them. Recently, we have begun searching for the totyq by taking in multiple citizens and asking each one separately about suspected totyq. As each is alone, they cannot plan together. If any one of them speaks the truth, we find the tot and punish the liars. If all of them could lie together, the lie would stand, but each knows someone else may tell the truth and cannot afford to lie.

Excerpt of a latter letter by the same author:

> There has been some discussion among our forces about whether Raxa should be king again. I oppose it. He was ineffective and of weak character, king only because Laxoxal wanted so. He failed to see the threat the totyq posed until they forced him to raise one of them. Next, he fled and hid for the entirety of the war. Had someone better been king, could the whole tragedy not have been avoided? It is clear that the citizens who once replaced him with a tot still do not like him or trust him. So, I advise, give them what they want: rule by the totyq, but not of Fecul. Appoint a Tot Cenuk who is loyal to you and worships the Moferrumyq Xalyq. May they be praised.
